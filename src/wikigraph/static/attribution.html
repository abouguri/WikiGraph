Sources & attribution

The offline teaching sample is authored synthetic data. Its revision IDs are fictional and its sentences are not Wikipedia evidence.

When the server uses Wikipedia snapshots, each evidence panel links to the original article revision. Article content is by Wikipedia contributors, distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license, subject to the source page’s applicable terms. Source revision histories identify contributors.

WikiGraph normalizes rendered text, removes reference markers, extracts links, and derives structured assertions. These transformations can introduce errors. Extraction rules are deliberately limited; inspect the source before relying on a connection.

The repository’s data/wikipedia directory contains the run manifest and revision-linked snapshots.
